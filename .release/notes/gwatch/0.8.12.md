# GWatch 0.8.12

GWatch 0.8.12 and GWatch Agent 0.8.8 ship together. Nothing new to set up: the
alerts and history you already have get more dependable.

## Changed

- **An alert that could not be sent is kept, and sent once it can be.** When an
  outage takes the internet connection with it, the e-mail or chat message
  about it now waits and is tried again for up to a day, in order, honouring
  `Retry-After`. An outage and its recovery that both missed arrive as one
  message saying how long it was down. Anything sent late says when it was
  raised, and Monitor health shows what is waiting.
- **A failing check is confirmed in seconds**, not after several full
  intervals (Settings › Alerts › Re-check a failing check after; 30 seconds by
  default).
- **Flapping checks send one warning** instead of an alert per change.
- **Latency percentiles at every range**: charts offer the 95th and 99th
  percentile from 3 days to a year.
- **A failure at the name lookup says so**, and points at the DNS server.

## Fixed

- **Six-day certificates were always "expiring."** They now warn once a third
  of their lifetime is left; longer certificates are unaffected.
- **A full disk made every check run once a second** (#211). Checks now back
  off, and Monitor health says results are not being saved.
- **One slow disk no longer holds everything up** (#210): saving a result no
  longer blocks the scheduler and the dashboards.
